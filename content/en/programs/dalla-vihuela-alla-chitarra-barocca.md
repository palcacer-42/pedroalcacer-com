## Corde, Pelli e Passioni
### Dalla Vihuela alla Chitarra Barocca

**Vihuela and Baroque Guitar with Historical Percussion**

{{% program_description %}}

A journey through an era when music knew no borders and melodies, instruments and rhythms travelled between courts, cities and territories, giving life to a continuous encounter between cultures. The path begins with the vihuela de mano, the instrument that more than any other represents the Siglo de Oro of Spanish musical culture.

### Notes

The vihuela repertoire constitutes one of the most precious treasures of the Iberian Renaissance. The seven principal collections published between 1536 and 1576 tell of a most refined musical civilisation, in which fantasy, improvisation and instrumental practice were an integral part of the musical experience.

Alongside the vihuela, the programme leads towards Italy and towards the baroque guitar, the instrument that at the beginning of the Seicento progressively began to replace the vihuela, bringing with it a new language made of rhythms, harmonies and virtuosity. Historical percussion completes this sonorous universe, bringing to the foreground the dimension of dance, rhythm and improvisation.

The dialogue between Spain and Italy is at the heart of the entire project. Already in the Cinquecento, musicians and repertoires continually crossed the Mediterranean and European courts. Vihuela, baroque guitar and percussion thus meet in a concert that restores to the audience not only the music of the past, but its most vivid and surprising character.

### Biographies

#### Pedro Alcàcer Doria
*Vihuela e Chitarra barocca*

Pedro was born in 1982 in Mexico City and began his musical education early, influenced by his father, the Catalan jazz musician Francesc Alcàcer. He studied jazz guitar with Francisco Lelo de la Rea and subsequently classical guitar and composition with Héctor Ramos. He completed his classical guitar studies at the renowned "Escuela Nacional de Música-UNAM" and continued with prominent teachers such as Isabelle Villei, Eloy Cruz and Antonio Corona, specialising in Renaissance and Baroque repertoire. In 2006 he moved to Barcelona, Spain, where he obtained the professional diploma for Renaissance and Baroque plucked string instruments at the Conservatory of Girona with the renowned teacher Xavier Díaz-Latorre. He then continued his studies at the Hochschule für Künste Bremen, studying lute with Joachim Held and Lee Santana. He has collaborated with important conductors such as Gabriel Garrido, Alessandro di Marchi, Marco Mencoboni, Paolo Faldi, Riccardo Doni, Horacio Franco, Christoph Hammer, Carlos Aranzay, Gerhard Oppel, Burak Özdemir and others, performing at prestigious festivals in Italy, Germany, Spain, France, Czech Republic, Belgium, Netherlands, Brazil and Mexico. He currently lives in Berlin and collaborates with various early music ensembles across Europe.

#### Matteo Rabolini
*Percussioni*

Born in Genoa on 30 August 1980, he graduated with top marks in Percussion Instruments at the Paganini Conservatory of Genoa with a thesis on Historical Percussion. Since 2006 he has devoted himself to the study of early music and applied percussion, attending courses and masterclasses with important teachers including Pedro Estevan. He has collaborated with Holland Baroque, Bremer Baroque Orchestra, L'Arpeggiata, Les Talens Lyriques, I Talenti Vulcanici, Arte dei Suonatori, Orkiestra Historyczna, La Risonanza, Modo Antiquo, Accademia Degli Astrusi, Wrocław Baroque Orchestra, Academia Montis Regalis, Teatro Carlo Felice, Orchestra Sinfonica G. Verdi di Milano, Orchestra Sinfonica di Sanremo and others. He has performed at important festivals including the Handel Festival in Halle, MiTo, Bremen Music Festival and Bach Festival in Leipzig. He has recorded for Warner, Sony Classical, Dynamic, Tactus and Pentatone. As a collector he owns more than 400 instruments (mainly percussion), ancient and modern, from around the world.
